## Overview

This repository hosts the public institutional landing page for **Auracelle AI Governance Labs** — a strategic cognition and governance stress-testing suite purpose-built for defense ministries, international organizations, intelligence agencies, standards bodies, frontier AI developers, and policy research institutes.

Auracelle surfaces governance failure modes before policy implementation through structured wargaming, multi-agent simulation, adversarial shock injection, and empirical after-action reporting — all grounded in proprietary multi-stratum intelligence architecture.

## The Suite

Auracelle comprises three simulation modules sharing a single governance intelligence layer:

### ⬡ Auracelle Charlie — Module Alpha
*AI Governance Policy Stress-Testing*

Interrogates institutional coordination, adoption dynamics, legitimacy thresholds, and evidence standards under adversarial and multi-stakeholder conditions.

- **Framework:** E-AGPO-HT v3.1
- **Mechanics:** Policy wargaming · Red teaming · External shock injection · Multi-actor negotiation
- **Version:** v3 · Research Prototype · Streamlit + API
- **Scenarios:** 7+ (frontier AI regulation, autonomous weapons, data governance, AI safety treaty, and more)
- **Output:** After-Action Reports (AAR) · JSON/CSV session exports

### ⬡ Auracelle Orion — Module Beta
*Cyber Governance Escalation & Resilience*

Models cross-sector incident response, supply chain trust dependencies, and decision thresholds for emergency powers and disclosure obligations.

- **Framework:** E-AGPO-HT v4
- **Mechanics:** Cyber escalation · External shock injection · Supply chain trust · Emergency powers thresholds
- **Version:** v4 · Accepted: Geneva Cyber Week 2026 (May 4–8) · NATO-demo ready
- **Scenarios:** 6 (critical infrastructure attack, ransomware escalation, attribution dispute, and more)
- **Output:** After-Action Reports (AAR)

*Assurance & Standards Resilience*

Evaluates verification regimes and confidence calibration under constrained information and institutional stress across nuclear, biotech, space, and frontier AI domains.

## Architecture: Public Core · Private Edge

Auracelle is designed around a deliberate dual-use separation:

| Layer | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Public** | Transparent, citable, academically rigorous — open methodology for peer review, grant funding, and conference deployment. Grounded in Auracelle AI Governance Labs research and UC Berkeley CLTC affiliation. |
| **Private** | Configured deployments of the same engine — tailored to client context under NDA. Clients access expertise and deployment, not the underlying IP. |

> *"We keep the methodology and measurement framework open and auditable, while tailoring scenarios, data, and decision matrices to client context under NDA."*

## Frameworks

### E-AGPO-HT — Evans Accelerated Governance Policy Optimisation, Hierarchical Theory
Powers **Charlie** and **Orion**. A formal multi-stratum mathematical architecture for quantifiable, testable AI governance policy evaluation:

```
Stratum III  →  g-GWC  (Global Wargaming Coefficient)
Stratum II   →  7 BGC  (Boundary Governance Capabilities: STI · SAD · ESI · NDM · SRA · IIC · ASI)
                + 4 ACC AI Factors
Stratum I    →  ~40 NOF  (Negotiation Operational Factors)
```

### E-AGSO-HT — Evans Accelerated Governance Standards Optimisation, Hierarchical Theory
Powers **Lyra**. A parallel multi-stratum framework adapted for assurance and standards verification in high-risk domains — evaluates whether standards regimes maintain integrity under institutional stress, constrained information, and adversarial pressure.

> Full framework documentation, scoring constructs, and simulation parameters are available to institutional partners, grant-funded research collaborators, and verified consulting clients under executed NDA.
